BudVia

Privacy policy

Effective 10 September 2026. Publisher: Tahsin Sakin. Product: BudVia.

What BudVia does not do

BudVia does not create accounts, run a server, include analytics or advertising SDKs, or sell tickets. It does not collect location. Directions open the maps app already on the phone.

What stays on the device

Trip text lives only on that device: localStorage key belvia-v2 in the PWA, or app storage in the native build. There is no BudVia backend to receive it.

What leaves when you tap

Ticket rows open a third-party app or that company’s public website. Directions open the system maps app or OpenStreetMap. LinkedIn opens LinkedIn. Those destinations have their own policies.

Permissions

The Android build requests no location, camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, or extra storage permissions.

Deletion

Clear the trip in the app, uninstall the app, or clear site data for this origin. That is the only copy.
