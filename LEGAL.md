## Data and cookies

- No account. No BudVia server. No analytics SDK.
- Trip text stays in `localStorage` key `belvia-v2` on that phone.
- No marketing cookies. No cookie banner. Necessary storage only.
- Forms in the app are local notes, not a signup.

## Third parties that can load

- unpkg.com Leaflet 1.9.4 (map script and CSS)
- tile.openstreetmap.org when the Places map is opened
- Wizz, FlixBus, Airbnb, Booking, maps, LinkedIn if the user taps those links

Those parties have their own policies.
