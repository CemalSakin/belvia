Cookie policy

Effective 13 September 2026.

BudVia does not set advertising or analytics cookies. There is no cookie consent pop-up because there is no optional cookie to accept.

Storage the app needs

The trip list is saved in browser localStorage under belvia-v2. That is the plan you typed. It is not used to track you across sites.

A one-time bag hint can use sessionStorage so the sheet does not return in the same visit.

If you open the map

Leaflet and OpenStreetMap tiles load from third-party hosts. Those hosts may set their own cookies. That happens only after you open Places.

How to clear

Use Clear this phone, or clear site data for tahsinsakin.github.io in the browser.
